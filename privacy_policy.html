Privacy Policy

✦ Effective Date: March 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MyHealth ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency and care.

1 Information We Collect

a. Personal Information You Provide

  • User Content: Any data you explicitly enter into the App, such as workout logs, exercises, and body weight entries.

b. Health Data (HealthKit)

  • If you grant permission, the App can read and write data from the Apple HealthKit database (e.g., body weight).
  • This data is used to sync your health metrics with the App and provide a unified view of your fitness progress.
🛡️

We do not use health data for marketing or advertising purposes.

c. Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you interact with the App (e.g., features used, time spent).
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your mobile device (e.g., model, operating system version).

2 How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • Improve and personalize your user experience.
  • Respond to your inquiries or support requests.

3 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4 Your Rights and Choices

  • Data Deletion: You can delete all your associated data directly within the App settings by selecting "Delete Data".
  • HealthKit Permissions: You can revoke the App's access to HealthKit at any time through your iOS device's Privacy settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health).

5 Security

We implement reasonable security measures, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6 Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the effective date above.
