Privacy Policy

✦ Effective Date: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MyHealth ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). MyHealth is designed to store your data locally on your device — we do not require an account and we do not operate a server that stores your personal data.

1 Information We Collect

🔒

MyHealth does not require you to create an account. All of your workout, exercise, and body weight data is stored locally in a database on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any server we operate.

a. Personal Information You Provide

  • User Content: Data you explicitly enter into the App, such as workout logs, exercises, routines, and body weight entries. This is stored on your device only.
  • Progress Photos: Photos you take or select to attach to workouts or track visual progress. These are stored on your device and, if you choose, saved to your device's photo library. We do not upload these photos anywhere.

b. Health Data (Apple HealthKit)

  • If you grant permission, the App can read and write body weight data from the Apple HealthKit database on your device.
  • This data is used only to sync your body weight between HealthKit and the App and stays on your device, subject to Apple's HealthKit rules.
🛡️

We do not use health data for marketing or advertising purposes, and this data never leaves your device through the App.

c. Camera, Microphone & Photo Library

  • With your permission, the App uses your camera and microphone to let you capture photos or short videos for exercises and progress tracking, and your photo library to select or save progress pictures.

d. Notifications

  • With your permission, the App can send local notifications (e.g., workout reminders, long-workout alerts). These are scheduled on your device and are not sent through a remote push service.

e. Live Activities (iOS)

  • On supported iOS devices, the App can display a Live Activity on your Lock Screen while a workout is in progress, showing your current workout status. This runs entirely on-device.

2 How We Use Your Information

Because your data is stored locally, we use it only to power the App's features on your device:

  • Track and display your workouts, exercises, routines, and progress.
  • Sync body weight with Apple HealthKit, if enabled.
  • Send workout reminder notifications, if enabled.

3 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, and since MyHealth does not transmit your workout data to us, we have nothing to share. The only exception:

  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information in our possession (such as support correspondence) if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4 Your Rights and Choices

  • Data Deletion: You can delete all data stored by the App directly within Settings by selecting "Delete Data." Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the App also removes all associated data from your device.
  • HealthKit Permissions: You can revoke the App's access to HealthKit at any time through your iOS device's Privacy settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health).
  • Camera, Photos & Notifications: You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

5 Security

Your data is stored in a local database on your device. We recommend using your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, encryption) to protect access to your device and its contents.

6 Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App inappropriately, please contact us.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add features like optional account sync in the future. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy at this page and updating the effective date above.
